Privacy policy

n8n assistant is a personal application used only by its owner.

It accesses the owner's own Google account (Gmail and Google Calendar) to automate personal tasks such as sorting mail and managing events.

Data is processed on the owner's own server. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data from other users is collected.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
